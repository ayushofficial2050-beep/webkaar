How to reduce image size?

  1. Click on "Choose File" or drag & drop your image.
  2. Select your desired Output Format (WEBP is recommended).
  3. Adjust the Quality Slider (Lower % means smaller size).
  4. Preview the "Before" and "After" size instantly.
  5. Click Download Image to save your file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does compressing an image reduce its quality? +
Our smart compression algorithm removes unnecessary data from the image file while preserving visual clarity. Unless you set the quality slider very low, the difference is usually unnoticeable to the human eye.
Which format is best for website speed? +
WEBP is the highly recommended format. It provides superior compression compared to JPG and PNG, making your website load faster and improving your Google PageSpeed scores.

About Image Compressor

  • 100% Private: Images are compressed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.
  • WEBP Format: Recommended for the highest compression with best quality for modern websites.
  • Transparency: Converting a transparent PNG to JPG will automatically give it a white background.
