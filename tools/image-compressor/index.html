WebKaar Image Compressor is a smart tool that helps you reduce the file size of your photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP) by up to 80% while maintaining the best quality. Whether you need to upload a photo for a government form, optimize your website speed, or save storage space, this tool does it instantly.

Why choose WebKaar Compressor?

  • 100% Privacy: Unlike other sites, your photos are compressed locally in your browser. No image is ever uploaded to a server.
  • Custom Quality & Format: Use the slider to balance file size and visual quality. Easily convert between WEBP, JPG, and PNG formats.
  • Unlimited & Free: Compress as many images as you want without any restrictions or watermarks.

How to reduce image size in KB?

  1. Click on "Choose File" or drag & drop your image.
  2. Select your desired Output Format (WEBP is best for websites).
  3. Adjust the Compression Quality Slider (Lower % means smaller size).
  4. Preview the "Before" and "After" size instantly.
  5. Click Download Image to save your optimized file.
