How to Remove Watermarks

Upload your image, draw rectangles over all watermark areas, then tap Remove. The tool uses smart patch matching to fill the selected area using the best matching surrounding texture.

Works Best For

  • Text watermarks on plain or gradient backgrounds
  • Corner logo watermarks
  • Date and timestamp overlays
  • Semi-transparent watermarks

100% Private

Your image never leaves your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using Canvas API. No server, no storage.

How to Use

Step 1: Upload your image.

Step 2: Draw a rectangle over the watermark by dragging.

Step 3: Draw more rectangles if needed.

Step 4: Tap "Remove Watermark".

Step 5: Download as PNG or JPG.


Tip: Draw selection slightly larger than the watermark for best results.

Undo: Removes the last drawn selection.

Reset: Restores the original image.


Privacy: Image never leaves your device. 100% browser-based.
