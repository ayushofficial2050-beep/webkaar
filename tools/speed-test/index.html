How Fast Is Your Internet?

Use the WebKaar Internet Speed Test to check your exact download speed, upload speed, ping and jitter — in seconds. Works on Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL, WiFi, and any broadband connection. No app needed, no login, completely free.

Understanding Your Results

  • Download Speed: How fast data comes to your device. Affects streaming, browsing, downloads.
  • Upload Speed: How fast data goes from your device. Affects video calls, sending files.
  • Ping: Round-trip time in ms. Lower is better. Critical for gaming and video calls.
  • Jitter: Variation in ping. Low jitter = stable connection.

What is a Good Speed in India?

Jio 4G averages 20–45 Mbps. Airtel 4G averages 25–60 Mbps. A good home broadband for a family is 100+ Mbps. For gaming, aim for ping under 40ms.

FAQ

What is a good ping for gaming? Under 20ms is excellent. 20–50ms is good. Above 100ms causes lag.

What is jitter? Variation in ping over time. Under 10ms is stable. High jitter causes choppy video calls.

Does speed test use a lot of data? Each test uses approximately 20–40MB — similar to 2 minutes of HD video streaming.
