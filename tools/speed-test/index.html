Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good internet speed in India? +
For basic browsing and social media, 5–10 Mbps is sufficient. For HD streaming or video calls, 15–25 Mbps is recommended. If you stream in 4K or play competitive games, aim for 50+ Mbps. (Note: Jio and Airtel 4G usually average 20–40 Mbps).
What does Ping and Jitter mean? +
Ping measures the reaction time of your connection (how fast you get a response after sending a request). A ping under 20ms is excellent.

Jitter is the variation in your ping over time. A high jitter means your connection is unstable, which causes lag in games and stuttering in video calls.
