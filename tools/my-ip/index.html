Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my location not 100% exact? +
IP geolocation maps to your ISP's routing center or data facility, not your physical GPS coordinates. This is a standard architectural feature of the internet designed to protect individual user privacy while providing general regional accuracy.
What does ASN mean? +
ASN stands for Autonomous System Number. It is a unique, globally assigned identifier given to your internet service provider (ISP) or network operator. It helps routers worldwide understand which network block your IP address belongs to.
Does this tool save my IP history? +
No, we do not save your IP history on our servers. Your IP history is saved entirely in your browser's local storage for your personal convenience. If you clear your browser data or click the 'Clear' button, it is permanently deleted.
