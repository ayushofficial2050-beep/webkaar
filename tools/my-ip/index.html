What is My IP Address?

Your IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique identifier assigned to your device when you connect to the internet. WebKaar IP Checker Pro shows your public IPv4 and IPv6 address, detects VPN/Proxy/Tor usage, displays your ISP and ASN routing info, and tests your connection latency to major global servers.

What is a VPN Detector?

Our VPN/Proxy/Tor detection uses trusted IP intelligence databases to check if your connection is being routed through a virtual private network, proxy server, or the Tor anonymity network.

Why is the location not exact?

IP addresses reflect your ISP's routing server location, not your exact GPS coordinates. For mobile users, this often shows a major city in your state. This protects your privacy.

What is ASN?

An Autonomous System Number (ASN) identifies a network operator or ISP. It shows which organization controls the IP block your address belongs to.
