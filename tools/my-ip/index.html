What is My IP Address?

Your IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique number assigned to your device when you connect to the internet. WebKaar IP Checker allows you to see your public IPv4/IPv6 address, identify your Internet Service Provider (ISP), and check your network geolocation on a map.

Why is the location not exact?

IP addresses show the location of your ISP's server routing your data, not your exact GPS coordinates. For mobile data users, this often shows a major city in your state instead of your specific town. This is completely normal and protects your privacy!

About IP Checker

  • Privacy First: We do not log or save your IP address.
  • ISP Location: Shows your network's server location, not exact GPS.
  • Fingerprint: Displays the OS and Browser data your device shares.
