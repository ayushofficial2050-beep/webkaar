About Age & Death Calculator

The WebKaar Age & Death Calculator is an advanced utility that not only tells you your exact chronological age in years, months, and days but also gives you a theoretical estimation of your life expectancy based on biological and lifestyle factors.

How does the Death Calculator work?

While nobody can predict the exact date of passing, science shows that factors like gender, stress levels, and lifestyle habits significantly impact your lifespan. By selecting your health habits, our algorithm calculates an estimated age you might reach, helping you realize the value of time.
