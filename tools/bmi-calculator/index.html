Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI and how is it calculated? +
BMI (Body Mass Index) is a value derived from the mass and height of a person. It is calculated by dividing your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in meters (kg/m²). It helps identify weight categories that may lead to health problems.
Is the BMI Calculator perfectly accurate for everyone? +
While BMI is highly accurate for average adults, it has limitations. It does not distinguish between muscle mass and fat. Therefore, bodybuilders or athletes may have a high BMI without being unhealthy. It is also not suitable for pregnant women.
