Understanding BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple index of weight-for-height that is commonly used to classify underweight, overweight and obesity in adults. It provides a good indicator of healthy body weight based on your height.

BMI Categories

  • Underweight: BMI less than 18.5
  • Normal weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30 or greater

How is BMI Calculated?

Metric: BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²
Imperial: BMI = 703 × Weight (lbs) ÷ Height (in)²

What is Ideal Weight Range?

Ideal weight range is the weight span where your BMI falls between 18.5 and 24.9 — considered healthy for your height. This calculator shows you that range automatically after you calculate.
