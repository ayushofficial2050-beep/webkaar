Frequently Asked Questions

What does a 16:9 aspect ratio mean? +
An aspect ratio of 16:9 means that for every 16 units of width, there are 9 corresponding units of height. It is the standard widescreen format used for YouTube videos, HD Televisions, and most computer monitors (like 1920x1080 resolution).
Will this tool crop my image? +
No, this tool acts as a calculator. It helps you find the exact mathematical dimensions you need to resize your images or videos without stretching or distorting them. You can then use those numbers in your editing software.
